# OpenSmell — The Ask

**For labs, IRBs, hospital innovation offices, and scientific partners**

---

## What we want

We are looking for partners who can help **move OpenSmell from honest simulation to real chemical and human evidence** — without turning the work into diagnostic marketing.

### Preferred collaborations

1. **Sensor / mixture characterization**  
   Known VOC mixtures or headspace standards vs MQ-135-class response → honest proxy map.

2. **Research protocol co-design**  
   Non-diagnostic feasibility or ambient/breath-adjacent monitoring under IRB or equivalent ethics oversight.

3. **Reference anchoring**  
   Optional GC-MS or breath-biopsy-class labels on a subset of samples so software claims stay grounded.

4. **Institutional pilot (research terms)**  
   On-prem or local host, audit logs, human-in-the-loop alerts, client-owned data.

---

## What we are not asking for

- Endorsement that OpenSmell **diagnoses** disease  
- Permission to market “FDA approved” or clinical accuracy we do not have  
- Ownership of your patient or client biological data  
- Expansion of claims beyond screening-support without evidence
